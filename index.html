About

Built by students. Run like a fund.

Founded 2016 at USC's TKE Beta Sigma Chapter.

We exist to develop rigorous investors and compound the chapter's endowment for generations to come.

Analysts pitch. The committee debates thesis, valuation, catalysts, risk. Capital is allocated by vote.

Beyond the portfolio: an alumni mentorship program, a guest speaker series, and a partnership with St. Jude.
